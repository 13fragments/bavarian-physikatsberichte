The feeding of children in the first year of life takes place generally through mother's milk, although in the lower social classes solid foods, especially potatoes and flour dishes, are given early alongside it. The rapidly visible consequences of the latter mode of feeding are predisposition to and development of scrofula, rickets, and atrophy.
