## 6. Publication guardrails

- Do not claim that `Oberbayern` is covered by `BSB Cgm 6874`.
- Do not pool `all_deaths` percentages (e.g., `Karlstadt` `37.3%`, `Riedenburg` `54.78%`, `Scheinfeld` `21.88%`) with birth-cohort mortality rates (`live_births` or `total_births_incl_stillborn`).
- Do not claim causal proof from cross-sectional district averages alone; state the observed correlation alongside confounding factors noted by the physicians themselves (climate, illegitimate birth rates, housing, maternal field labor, and sanitation).
- Remove `[A]` markers only after independent review by a professional paleographer is complete.
