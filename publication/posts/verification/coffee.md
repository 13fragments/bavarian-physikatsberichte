# Angle `coffee`: substitute coffee as an everyday drink of the poor

I read every passage below on the page image, not in the machine text. The crops that I used are in `scratchpad/crops/coffee/`.

### bsb00140604_0051  (Dahn, Pfalz; bsb00140604 canvas 51; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00140604?page=51)
- Claim supported: "Coffee drinking" is the most widespread habit in the canton, the drink is named after coffee substitutes and not after Coffea arabica, and more than half of all meals consist of this coffee-coloured water with a little milk plus potatoes or bread. The page says nothing about the effect of the drink on health.
- German (ink): "Am allgemeinsten verbreitet ist das „Kaffeetrinken“, das jedoch nicht nach der Caffea arabica, sondern nach Kaffeesurrogaten so genannt wird. Man kann annehmen, daß mehr als die Hälfte der Mahlzeiten im Kantone aus solchem Kaffee-gefärbten Wasser mit etwas Milch – & Kartoffeln oder Brod bestehen."
- English: "Most widespread of all is 'coffee drinking', which, however, takes its name not from Coffea arabica but from coffee substitutes. One can assume that more than half of the meals in the canton consist of such coffee-coloured water with a little milk – and potatoes or bread."
- Corrections against the machine text: "Der allgemeinster" -> "Am allgemeinsten"; "Kaffentrinken" -> "Kaffeetrinken"; "Kaffeesüerrogaten" -> "Kaffeesurrogaten"; "in Kantone" -> "im Kantone"; "Wassen" -> "Wasser"; "Stolch" -> "Milch".
- Confidence: high (every word read)
- Caveats: "Man kann annehmen" marks the share as the physician's estimate, not a count. The meal consists of the drink together with potatoes or bread; it is not the drink alone. The physician does not name the substitutes (no chicory on this page). FAILED PART: he makes no statement on health effects of the drink. The next sentence only says that the Dahn people eat a lot of badly prepared food. I searched all 73 Dahn pages in the machine text and found no other coffee passage. Canvas 52 only names coffee beside brandy as a "national drink".

### bsb00145114_0123  (Kronach, Oberfranken; bsb00145114 canvas 123; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00145114?page=123)
- Claim supported: Chicory coffee is very common among the poorer class, and one merchant in Kronach sells 300 Centner of chicory in a year. The number 300 is clear in the ink.
- German (ink): "Die Familie zu Hause muß freilich nicht selten darben und sich mit Erdaepfeln und Cichorienkaffee begnügen. Letzteres Getraenk ist überhaupt unter der aermern Classe ein sehr allgemeines und es ist erstaunlich, welche Quantitaeten Cichorien die hiesigen Kaufleute das Jahr über verkaufen. So verkauft ein Kaufmann dahier 300 Centner in einem Jahre und außer diesem sind noch 8 Kaufleute hier, deren Geschaefte gleich gut gehen."
- English: "The family at home must, admittedly, not seldom go short and make do with potatoes and chicory coffee. This latter drink is altogether a very common one among the poorer class, and it is astonishing what quantities of chicory the local merchants sell in the course of the year. Thus one merchant here sells 300 hundredweight in one year, and besides him there are 8 more merchants here whose businesses do equally well."
- Corrections against the machine text: "Erdarpfeln" -> "Erdaepfeln"; "Letzleres" -> "Letzteres"; "vermerm" -> "aermern"; "Quantitativen" -> "Quantitaeten"; "Geschärfte" -> "Geschaefte"; the first "ver-kaufen" is struck out in the ink.
- Confidence: high (every word read)
- Caveats: The digits "300" and "8" are unambiguous. The physician gives no source for the sales number, so treat it as his report of local knowledge. A Bavarian Centner was 56 kg, so 300 Centner is about 17 tonnes; the page does not give a metric value. He says the 8 other businesses "do equally well"; he does not say that each sells 300 Centner. "The family at home" is the family of the raftsmen (Flößer), who eat sausage in the inn. This hand writes ä as "ae"; the ae/ä spelling is the only point of doubt.

### bsb00140611_0401  (Erlangen Land, Mittelfranken; bsb00140611 canvas 401; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00140611?page=401)
- Claim supported: At the dance, the young men treat their girls to beer and, when the girls have had enough of it, to coffee drunk from Maß beer mugs.
- German (ink): "Die Bursche traktiren ihre Maedchen bei dem Tanze mit Bier, und wenn sie davon genug haben, mit Kaffee aus Maaskrügen getrunken."
- English: "The lads treat their girls at the dance to beer and, when they have had enough of that, to coffee drunk from one-Maß beer mugs."
- Corrections against the machine text: none
- Confidence: high (every word read)
- Caveats: The context (canvas 400, machine text only) is the village church fair (Kirchweih) dance, so this is a festival custom, not daily life. The page does not say that this coffee was a substitute, and it does not mention poverty. A Bavarian Maß was about 1.07 litres.

### bsb00145138_0059  (Nürnberg, Mittelfranken; bsb00145138 canvas 59; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00145138?page=59)
- Claim supported: The rich man sips one or at most two cups of strong coffee in the morning; the poorer people drink a Seidlein, often more, of "their brown liquid" with the same pleasure.
- German (ink): "Während der Reiche eine, höchstens zwei Tassen allerdings starken Kaffees morgens schlürft, trinken die Aermeren ein Seidlein oft noch mehr ihrer braunen Flüssigkeit mit demselben Behagen."
- English: "While the rich man sips one, at most two cups of admittedly strong coffee in the morning, the poorer drink a Seidlein, often even more, of their brown liquid with the same enjoyment."
- Corrections against the machine text: none. The ink has "Seidlein", not "Seidel".
- Confidence: high (every word read)
- Caveats: I also read the lines before and after on the images. Canvas 58 (downloaded and read) gives the link to substitutes: "Das Frühstück besteht fast allgemein aus Kaffee, der selbst in den bürgerlichen Haushaltungen vielfach wegen der Färbung bei den ärmeren Klassen aber der Ersparung wegen stark mit Surrogaten verschiedener Art versetzt wird." The physician does not name chicory. He defends the habit: "[...] und doch läßt sich für die Bevölkerung einer großen Stadt kein anderes Frühstück finden, das wohlfeiler, wohlschmeckender, leichter zu bereiten und im Allgemeinen der Gesundheit zuträglicher wäre." That is his opinion. A Seidlein was about half a Maß, roughly half a litre; the page gives no volume.

### bsb00145146_0415  (Stadtprozelten, Unterfranken; bsb00145146 canvas 415; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00145146?page=415)
- Claim supported: On a poor household's dirty table stands a large pot of "coffee", which the physician defines as goat's milk with a brown brew made from "German coffee", beside steaming potatoes. The word "chicory" is NOT on the page.
- German (ink): "Auf dem schmutzigen Tische steht ein großer Hafen voll Kaffee's – Ziegenmilch mit einer braunen aus deutschem Kaffee bereiteten Brühe – und in einem alten Brodnapfe [?] erblicke ich eine Parthie dampfender Kartoffel. Salz ist für diesmal nicht vorhanden, denn es hat „an Kreuzern“ gemangelt, solches zu kaufen."
- English: "On the dirty table stands a large pot full of coffee – goat's milk with a brown brew prepared from German coffee – and in an old bread bowl [?] I see a portion of steaming potatoes. There is no salt this time, for 'the Kreuzer' to buy it were lacking."
- Corrections against the machine text: "Kaffei's" -> "Kaffee's"; "Zirgsmilch" -> "Ziegenmilch"; "ans deutschem Kaffee beuteten Leuse" -> "aus deutschem Kaffee bereiteten Brühe"; "Beadnuchte" -> "Brodnapfe [?]"; "Kartteffel" -> "Kartoffel"; "Satz" -> "Salz"; "gewanzelt" -> "gemangelt".
- Confidence: medium (one word marked [?]: the name of the vessel that holds the potatoes)
- Caveats: "Deutscher Kaffee" was a usual name of the time for coffee substitute, most often roasted chicory, but this physician does not say which plant. The post must not put "chicory" into his mouth; write "'German coffee', a coffee substitute". The milk is goat's milk. The scene is the physician's own eyewitness sketch of a typical poor room ("sehe ich als Arzt gar viele" follows in the machine text); the village names at the top of the page are Ober- or Unteraltenbuch and Neuenbuch. In the following lines (read on the image) 4 dirty, sickly children and both parents sit around the table.

### coffee_erbendorf_p227  (Erbendorf, Oberpfalz und Regensburg; bsb00041245 canvas 227; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00041245?page=227)
- Claim supported: Added page. The physician names the substitutes (chicory, mangel beet, carrots, roasted grain) and states that they often harm health. This page fills the gap that the Dahn page leaves.
- German (ink): "Häufig werden jedoch statt der Kaffeebohnen Surrogate als Cichorien, Runkelrüben, gelbe Rüben, gebranntes Korn hiezu mit verwendet, die aber gewiß keinen Ersatz für selbe bieten, und den ohnehin geringen Ernährungswerth des Kaffee's noch bedeutender vermindern und nicht selten nachtheilige Folgen für die Gesundheit haben."
- English: "Often, however, instead of coffee beans, substitutes such as chicory, mangel beets, carrots, and roasted grain are used as well, which certainly offer no replacement for the beans, reduce the already small nutritional value of coffee still more, and not seldom have harmful consequences for health."
- Corrections against the machine text: "Kaffenbohnen" -> "Kaffeebohnen"; "Rüken" -> "Rüben".
- Confidence: high (every word read)
- Caveats: The harm is the physician's opinion; he names no specific illness. Just before, he writes that in the town of Erbendorf nearly everyone, "von dem dürftigen, wie von dem Reichen", takes "Kaffee als Frühstück", and that coffee drinking spreads in the countryside, "namentlich von den Bauersfrauen". The top of the same page says that the usual morning meal is milk soup or water soup, so the page shows coffee beside soup, not a complete replacement.

### coffee_schillingsfuerst_p86  (Schillingsfürst, Mittelfranken; bsb00146478 canvas 86; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00146478?page=86)
- Claim supported: Added page. The physician gives the recipe: a few real beans, a handful of diced dried beet root, some chicory, boiled, with much milk and a little sugar, taken with black bread or boiled potatoes. He says coffee is no longer a luxury but a food.
- German (ink): "Es ist aber auch dieser Kaffe ein Gebräu eigener Art, das diesen Namen gar nicht oder kaum verdient. Denn zu einigen ächten Bohnen wird eine Hand voll gewürfelter und getrockneter Rangers-Wurzel [?] nebst etwas Cichorie genommen, und nach dem Abkochen mit viel Milch und etwas Zucker vermischt, und so getrunken, indem man dazu schwarzes Brod oder gesottene Kartoffeln ißt."
- English: "But this coffee is a brew of its own kind, which does not, or hardly, deserve the name. For to a few real beans one takes a handful of diced and dried mangel-beet root [?] together with some chicory, and after boiling mixes it with much milk and a little sugar, and drinks it so, eating black bread or boiled potatoes with it."
- Corrections against the machine text: "Bebräu" -> "Gebräu"; "Rangens-Wurzel" -> "Rangers-Wurzel [?]"; "genom̄en" is "genommen" written with a doubling stroke over the m; "ist" -> "ißt".
- Confidence: medium (one word marked [?]). The hand is pale and hasty. "Rangers" (a Franconian word for mangel beet) fits the strokes and the sense, but I cannot exclude "Rangens".
- Caveats: The sentence before reads in the ink: "Der Consumo des Kaffes ist hier ebenfalls ein starker, und letzterer hat längst aufgehört, ein Luxusartikel zu seyn, sondern er zählt mehr zu den Nahrungsmitteln." I read the middle of that sentence with confidence; the word "starker" at the line end I read with some doubt. The machine text continues that a plain milk or meat soup would be simpler and healthier ("Einfacher und gesünder wäre jedenfalls eine einfache [...] Suppe"). That sentence runs to the foot of the page and onto canvas 87, and I did NOT verify it on the image, so do not quote it.

## Summary

Claims that hold:
- Dahn: the "coffee" is made from substitutes, and by the physician's estimate more than half of all meals consist of it together with potatoes or bread.
- Kronach: chicory coffee is very common among the poorer class; one merchant sells 300 Centner of chicory a year. The number is correct.
- Erlangen Land: beer first, then coffee from Maß mugs at the dance. Word for word correct.
- Nürnberg: one or two cups of strong coffee for the rich, a Seidlein or more of "brown liquid" for the poor. Word for word correct; the ink says "Seidlein".

Claims that fail or need a change:
- Dahn, health effect: FAILS. The physician says nothing about the effect of the drink on health. Use the added Erbendorf page for a health statement, or the Nürnberg physician, who defends the coffee breakfast.
- Stadtprozelten, "chicory with milk": the ink says goat's milk with a brew of "deutschem Kaffee" (German coffee). Chicory is an interpretation, not his word. The scene itself holds.
- Erlangen Land shows a festival custom and does not mention substitutes or poverty, so it supports "coffee was everywhere", not "substitute coffee of the poor".

Strongest hook: Kronach. "It is astonishing what quantities of chicory the local merchants sell" – one merchant 300 Centner (about 17 tonnes) a year, with 8 more merchants doing equally well, while the raftsmen eat sausage in the inn and their families at home make do with potatoes and chicory coffee. A good second is the Schillingsfürst recipe: a few real beans, a handful of dried beet, some chicory, "a brew that hardly deserves the name".
