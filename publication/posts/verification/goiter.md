# Verification: goiter (*Kropf*) and cretinism

I read seven passages against the page images. Six hold. One fails: the Vilseck page names scrofula (*Scropheln*), not goiter. The Wiesentheid physician also names iodine and dismisses it; that is the strongest hook.

All images are in `data/verification_scans/posts/`. The crops that I read are in `scratchpad/crops/goiter/`. I downloaded three extra pages for context: Wiesentheid canvases 263 and 267, and Schillingsfürst canvas 44.

### goiter_uffenheim_p43  (Uffenheim, Mittelfranken; bsb00143120 canvas 43; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00143120?page=43)
- Claim supported: Yes, with one change of degree. The physician says that carrying heavy loads on the head is a "not uncommon" cause of goiter in women. He does not say "frequent".
- German (ink): "Das Heben schwerer Lasten gibt häufig Veranlassung zu Eingeweidebrüchen und beim weiblichen Geschlechte ist das Tragen schwerer Lasten auf dem Kopfe eine nicht seltene Ursache zur Entstehung des Kropfes und zu Lageveränderungen der Gebärmutter."
- English: "The lifting of heavy loads often gives rise to hernias, and in the female sex the carrying of heavy loads on the head is a not uncommon cause of the development of goiter and of displacements of the womb."
- Corrections against the machine text: "Eingewindebrüchen" -> "Eingeweidebrüchen"; "nicht selten Ursache" -> "nicht seltene Ursache".
- Confidence: high (every word read)
- Caveats: This is the physician's opinion about the cause. The word "häufig" (often) belongs to hernias, not to goiter. The sentence is in a section on the effects of hard farm work.

### goiter_eltmann_p362  (Eltmann, Unterfranken; bsb00140606 canvas 362; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00140606?page=362)
- Claim supported: Yes. In the town of Zeil, goiters are not rare, especially in women, and the physician thinks that heavy work, births, and carrying heavy loads may be the cause.
- German (ink): "Kröpfe und Strumen[?] sind besonders bei dem weiblichen Geschlechte nicht selten, was von schweren Arbeiten, Geburten, Tragen schwerer Lasten etc herkommen mag."
- English: "Goiters and strumas[?] are not rare, especially in the female sex, which may come from heavy work, births, the carrying of heavy loads, etc."
- Corrections against the machine text: "Septuen" -> "Strumen[?]"; "Lusten" -> "Lasten".
- Confidence: medium (one word marked [?])
- Caveats: The passage describes the town of Zeil only, under the heading "Zeil", not the whole Eltmann district. "herkommen mag" marks a guess by the physician. The doubtful second noun does not change the claim, because "Kröpfe" is clear. For a post, you can cut it: "Kröpfe [...] sind besonders bei dem weiblichen Geschlechte nicht selten, was von schweren Arbeiten, Geburten, Tragen schwerer Lasten etc herkommen mag."

### goiter_wiesentheid_p264  (Wiesentheid, Unterfranken; bsb00146483 canvas 264, with canvases 263 and 265 to 267; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00146483?page=264)
- Claim supported: Partly. The physician does give cretinism its own section, with the margin heading "Cretinismus." His stated reason is not the number of cretins. His reason is that one small, narrowly bounded area is the only home of the cretins in his region. That area is the three places Castell, Wiesenbronn, and Rüdenhausen.
- German (ink), opening, canvas 263 to 264: "Weil aber eben nur jener kleine und eng begränzte Bezirk die Heimath der Cretinen in unsrer Gegend ist, so darf eine nähere Erörterung der möglichen Ursache davon, so wie ein Blick auf die bisherige Lehre vom Cretinismus hier nicht umgangen werden!"
- English: "But because just that small and narrowly bounded district is the home of the cretins in our region, a closer discussion of its possible cause, and a look at the present doctrine of cretinism, must not be omitted here!"
- German (ink), iodine, canvas 265: "[...] der Jodgehalt der Luft und der Nahrungsmittel ist vorerst noch zweifelhaft, sein Einfluß aber jedenfalls untergeordnet."
- English: "[...] the iodine content of the air and of food is for now still doubtful, but its influence is in any case secondary."
- German (ink), his conclusion, canvas 267: "Bei uns ist ohne Zweifel der Kalkgehalt des Wassers ein wesentliches Moment und es ist nicht nöthig, den ersten Grund der Entstehung jener Bildungsanomalie wo anders zu suchen, als eben in dem harten Wasser, welches zumal in Castell und Wiesenbronn eine bekannte Calamität ist [...]"
- English: "With us the lime content of the water is without doubt an essential factor, and it is not necessary to look for the first cause of that malformation anywhere else than in the hard water, which is a known calamity, above all in Castell and Wiesenbronn [...]"
- His explanation in one sentence: He reviews the published theories (deep valleys, lack of light, stagnant air, lack of iodine, hard water, Virchow's theory of early fusion of the skull sutures from "territorial" causes), rejects the valley and sunlight theories because his three places are open and sunny, calls the role of iodine doubtful and secondary, and decides that the lime-rich hard drinking water of the three places is the cause.
- Corrections against the machine text: canvas 264 "Mangel an Jugendheit der Luft" -> "Mangel an Jodgehalt der Luft"; "Kaltgehalt" -> "Kalkgehalt"; "Wiesenbrunn" -> "Wiesenbronn"; canvas 265 "untergeordnete" -> "untergeordnet"; canvas 266 "Vischow" -> "Virchow", "denkennt den Gehalt der Brinkwasser" -> "erkennt den Gehalt der Trinkwasser"; canvas 267 "Bei ins" -> "Bei uns", "als aber in dem harten Wasser" -> "als eben in dem harten Wasser".
- Confidence: high (every word of the three quotes read)
- Caveats: All of this is the theory of his time. The list of causes that includes "Mangel an Jodgehalt der Luft und der Nahrungsmittel" (lack of iodine in air and food) is his report of another author, whom he cites from an 1858 journal; I read that author's name as "Ploss[?]" and did not verify the citation. I read canvases 265 and 266 in the key lines only, not word for word; the one-sentence summary uses the machine text for the rest. The section continues after canvas 267 (he adds a remark on heredity and conception in drunkenness on canvas 268, which I read in the machine text only).

### goiter_schillingsfuerst_p45  (Schillingsfürst, Mittelfranken; bsb00146478 canvas 45, sentence starts on canvas 44; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00146478?page=45)

- German (ink): "Körperlich und geistig verkümmert ist aber jenes Gebiet der Tauberströmung[?], in welchem die Gypsformation vorherrschend ist, wie die obere Taubergegend und das Wohnbach-Thal, indem hier der Cretinismus in allen seinen Variationen vorkömmt, und, wie schon gesagt, auf die Befreiung vom Militärdienste wegen Gebrechen Einfluß übt."
- English: "But physically and mentally stunted is that area of the Tauber drainage[?] in which the gypsum formation dominates, such as the upper Tauber region and the Wohnbach valley, since cretinism occurs here in all its variations and, as already said, influences exemption from military service on grounds of infirmity."
- Corrections against the machine text: "Fauverströmung" -> "Tauberströmung[?]"; "Hypo-formation" -> "Gypsformation"; "Laubergegend" -> "Taubergegend".
- Confidence: medium (one word marked [?])
- Caveats: The physician links cretinism to gypsum geology; that is his association, not a proven cause. "wie schon gesagt" points to an earlier page with the military figures, which I did not check. The page gives no numbers. The ink writes "verküm̄ert" and "vorköm̄t" with a doubling stroke over the m; I expanded it to "mm".

### goiter_weiler_p24  (Weiler, Schwaben; bsb00028867 canvas 24; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00028867?page=24)
- Claim supported: Yes. Women are often disfigured by fat necks and goiters; the physician knows no case of full cretinism, but knows some families in which all the children come close to it.
- German (ink): "Auffallend starke Entwicklung der Halsdrüsen findet man selten beim männlichen Geschlecht, während die dem lymphatischen Habitus hinneigenden Weiber häufig von Fetthälsen und Kröpfen entstellt sind. Von ausgebildetem Cretinismus ist mir übrigens kein Fall bekannt, wohl gibt es einzelne Familien, wo bereits sämtliche Kinder dem Cretinismus sich annähern."
- English: "A strikingly strong development of the neck glands is rarely found in the male sex, while the women, who tend toward the lymphatic habitus, are often disfigured by fat necks and goiters. For the rest, I know of no case of fully developed cretinism, but there are some families in which nearly all the children approach cretinism."
- Corrections against the machine text: "sterke" -> "starke"; "lymphotischen" -> "lymphatischen"; "Setthälfen" -> "Fetthälsen"; "so bereits" -> "wo bereits".
- Confidence: high (every word read)
- Caveats: "bereits" here is the regional (Swabian) sense "nearly", so "nearly all the children". "Lymphatic habitus" is a constitution theory of the time. The physician underlines "mir": no case known to him. I could not tell if the ink has "sämtliche" or "sämmtliche".

- Corrections against the machine text: "Cretinismusnahe" -> "Cretinismus nahe".

### goiter_vilseck_p33  (Vilseck, Oberpfalz; bsb00085711 canvas 33; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00085711?page=33)
- Claim supported: No, not for goiter. The ink does not say "Kröpfe". It says "Scropheln" (scrofula, a tuberculous swelling of the neck glands). The claim about impure, wet air, tuberculosis, and intermittent fever holds.
- German (ink): "[...] denn in und um Vilseck ist die Luft unrein, naß und im hohen Grade ungesund; weßhalb hier auch Scropheln[?], Tuberkulose und insbesondere Wechselfieber, meist mit Tertiantypus, mit außerordentlicher Intensität auftreten."
- English: "[...] for in and around Vilseck the air is impure, wet, and in a high degree unhealthy; which is why scrofula, tuberculosis, and above all intermittent fever, mostly of the tertian type, occur here with extraordinary intensity."
- Corrections against the machine text: "Kropfeln" -> "Scropheln[?]" (this changes the disease); "Kliseck" -> "Vilseck" (all occurrences); "4–8 Stunden" -> "4–5 Stunden".
- Confidence: medium for the quote (one word marked [?]); low for the goiter claim (not fit to publish as goiter)
- Caveats: The [?] is about the spelling only ("Scropheln" or "Skropheln"). The word starts with the same capital S as "Stadt" and "Stunden" on the same page, has no umlaut mark, and has no "pf". It is not "Kröpfe" or "Kröpfeln". Disease from bad air is the theory of the time; the intermittent fever is malaria, and the page goes on to blame the many ponds around Vilseck. Do not use this page in a goiter post. It fits a malaria or "bad air" post.

## Summary

Six claims hold: Uffenheim, Eltmann, Wiesentheid, Schillingsfürst, Weiler, and Rothenfels. Three need a small change of wording:

- Uffenheim says "a not uncommon cause", not "a frequent cause".
- Eltmann describes the town of Zeil only.
- Wiesentheid gives cretinism its own section because the cretins of his region live in one small area (Castell, Wiesenbronn, Rüdenhausen), not because of their number.

One claim fails: Vilseck. The machine text "Kropfeln" is a misreading of "Scropheln" (scrofula). The page does not mention goiter.

The strongest hook is Wiesentheid. In 1860 the physician had the right answer on his desk. He reports a published theory that names "Mangel an Jodgehalt der Luft und der Nahrungsmittel" (lack of iodine in air and food) as a cause of cretinism. Then he sets it aside: "der Jodgehalt der Luft und der Nahrungsmittel ist vorerst noch zweifelhaft, sein Einfluß aber jedenfalls untergeordnet." He blames the hard, lime-rich well water instead. The machine text hid this, because it read "Jodgehalt" as "Jugendheit" on canvas 264. A good second quote for the contrast is Uffenheim: carrying heavy loads on the head as a cause of goiter in women.
