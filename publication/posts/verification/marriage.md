# Verification: marriage permits, illegitimate births, and emigration

I read all six passages on the page images of BSB Cgm 6874. All six claims hold. The machine text had several wrong words; the corrections are listed for each passage. One of them changes the meaning (Hersbruck: "Unterkommen", not "Unternehmen").

### marriage_viechtach_p76  (Viechtach, Niederbayern; bsb00145143 canvas 76; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00145143?page=76)
- Claim supported: The physician names the municipalities' unconditional veto over marriage permits as the apparent cause of the many illegitimate children, and says that honest people are refused out of personal enmity while rogues are feared and get their settlement permit.
- German (ink): "Die Sittlichkeit scheint immer mehr in Verfall zu kommen, denn das Kammerfenstergehen ist allgemein gebräuchlich, und daß es nicht dabei bleibt, zeigen die vielen außerehelichen Kinder. Die Ursache dieses Übelstandes ist, wie es scheint, in dem unbedingten veto der Gemeinden in Heirathbewilligungen zu suchen. – Manche ehrliche Leute, die sich gut fortbringen könnten, läßt man nicht heirathen, weil ihnen der eine oder der andere der Gemeindebevollmächtigten Feind ist; Lumpen hingegen fürchtet man und diese setzen ihre Ansäßigmachung eher durch."
- English: "Morality seems to decline more and more, for going to the chamber window is a general custom, and the many illegitimate children show that it does not stop there. The cause of this evil is, as it seems, to be found in the unconditional veto of the municipalities in marriage permits. – Many honest people who could make a good living are not allowed to marry, because one or another of the municipal representatives is their enemy; rogues, however, are feared, and these get their settlement permit sooner."
- Corrections against the machine text: "fortbeinig-en" -> "fortbringen"; "Gemeindebevollmächte-igten" -> "Gemeindebevollmächtigten". Other words agree.
- Confidence: high (every word read)
- Caveats: This is the physician's opinion; he hedges twice ("scheint", "wie es scheint"). He gives no numbers. "Kammerfenstergehen" is the rural custom of night visits by young men at a girl's window. "veto" is written in Latin script. "Heirathbewilligungen" may carry a linking "s" ("Heirathsbewilligungen"); the ink does not settle this, and the meaning is the same. The quote is longer than three sentences; for a post, the last two sentences carry the claim.

### marriage_hersbruck_p136  (Hersbruck, Mittelfranken; bsb00109173 canvas 136; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00109173?page=136)
- Claim supported: The emigrants to America were, as a rule, people who found no livelihood in the district and therefore could not marry; not all of them found their fortune there, and that news ended the wish to follow them.
- German (ink): "Die bisherigen Auswanderer nach Amerika sind in der Regel solche, welche im Bezirke kein Unterkommen fanden, und darum nicht heirathen konnten. – Alle haben aber auch dort ihr Glück nicht gefunden, und diese Nachricht hat die Lust zur Nachfolge benommen."
- English: "The emigrants to America so far are as a rule those who found no place to make a living in the district, and therefore could not marry. – But not all of them found their fortune there either, and this news has taken away the desire to follow them."
- Corrections against the machine text: "kein Unternehmen" -> "kein Unterkommen". This correction matters: the word means a place or livelihood, not a business.
- Confidence: high (every word read)
- Caveats: "Alle haben ... nicht gefunden" means "not all found", not "none found". The physician does not name the marriage permit here; he links emigration to the lack of a livelihood, which was the legal condition for the permit. The statement is his general observation, without numbers.

### marriage_marktheidenfeld_p179  (Marktheidenfeld, Unterfranken; bsb00145124 canvas 179; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00145124?page=179)
- Claim supported: The only emigrants were a few people who could not marry at home, wanted to keep their promises of marriage, and went to America to marry there; the physician says that there will be no more "Franconian American women" once the poor are allowed to marry.
- German (ink): "Und nur einige, die sich hierorts nicht heurathen konnten und ihren Versprechungen doch treu bleiben wollten, haben um die Ehe dort einzugehen, sich nach Amerika begeben. Sie gedenken in Liebe ihrer alten Heimath, und wünschten [?] sich sogar zurück. Wird einst den Armen das Heurathen erlaubt werden, so wird es keine fränkischen Amerikanerinnen mehr geben."
- English: "And only a few, who could not marry here and yet wanted to stay true to their promises, went to America to enter into marriage there. They remember their old homeland with love, and would [?] even wish themselves back. When one day the poor are allowed to marry, there will be no more Franconian American women."
- Corrections against the machine text: "Kannten" -> "konnten"; "Wohsprechungen" -> "Versprechungen"; "gedanken" -> "gedenken"; "wünschten" is uncertain (it may be "wünschen"). The ink has "fränkischen Amerikanerinnen" (with -n), not "fränkische".
- Confidence: medium (one word marked [?]; the hand is hasty, but the last sentence, which carries the claim, is clear)
- Caveats: The sentence before the quote says that the inhabitant accepts no reasons for emigration and is content in and with his country. So the physician presents emigration as rare here. The earlier sentences say "einige" (a few people) without gender; only the last sentence makes them women. The physician's statement is an opinion and a prediction.

### marriage_windsheim_p42  (Windsheim, Mittelfranken; bsb00143119 canvas 42; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00143119?page=42)
- Claim supported: Far more marriages would take place if the settlement permit were not so hard to get; most men marry at 30 to 40 years, most women at 25 to 30 years.
- German (ink): "Ein Hang zur Ehelosigkeit ist nicht vorhanden, und es würden weit mehr Ehen eingegangen werden, wenn die Ansässigmachung nicht so sehr erschwert wäre. – Die meisten männlichen Personen heirathen im Alter von 30–40 Jahren, die meisten weiblichen im Alter von 25–30 Jahren."
- English: "There is no inclination to remain unmarried, and far more marriages would be entered into if settlement were not made so very difficult. – Most male persons marry at the age of 30–40 years, most female persons at the age of 25–30 years."
- Corrections against the machine text: "Helosigkeit" -> "Ehelosigkeit". In the next sentence, "mittelsmäßige" -> "mittelmäßige".
- Confidence: high (every word read; all four numbers are clear)
- Caveats: The ages are the physician's estimate, not a count. "Hang zur Ehelosigkeit" is underlined in the ink as a topic heading. The next sentence says that the fertility of marriages is moderate.

### marriage_neustadt_p138  (Neustadt an der Waldnaab, Oberpfalz; bsb00092700 canvas 138, sentence starts on canvas 137; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00092700?page=138)
- Claim supported: The municipalities often use their absolute right of objection against the settlement requests of "fallen" women to their own disadvantage, because after a refusal the municipality must support mother and children when they become destitute.
- German (ink): "Von dem absoluten Widerspruchsrechte, welches den Gemeinden bei den Ansässigmachungsgesuchen solcher Personen zusteht, machen dieselben öfters zu ihrem eigenen Nachtheile fleißig Gebrauch, denn wenn die Verehelichung solcher gefallenen Personen verweigert wird, so verbleibt meistens den Gemeinden im Falle gänzlicher Verarmung sowohl die Unterstützung der Mutter, als der Kinder, während bei der Gestattung sich verehelichen zu dürfen, dem Ehemanne die Hauptsorge für seine Angehörigen obliegt."
- English: "The municipalities often make diligent use, to their own disadvantage, of the absolute right of objection that they have against the settlement requests of such persons; for when marriage is refused to such fallen persons, the support of both the mother and the children mostly remains with the municipalities in the case of complete destitution, whereas, when permission to marry is given, the main care for his dependants lies with the husband."
- Corrections against the machine text: "Ehenmanie" -> "Ehemanne". The first five words ("Von dem absoluten Widerspruchs-") are the last line of canvas 137; I downloaded that page and read them there.
- Confidence: high (every word read)
- Caveats: "Solcher Personen" refers to the sentence before, on canvas 137: unmarried women, mainly in towns and markets, who found no match because they had no property, or who "durch ihr ausschweifendes Leben mehrere außereheliche Kinder erworben haben". "Fallen" is the physician's moral term. The quote is one long sentence; for a post, cut it with "[...]" after "Gebrauch". The physician adds that such married persons are often able to save some property and to raise their children properly. This is his argument, not a statistic.

### marriage_nittenau_p50  (Nittenau, Oberpfalz; bsb00060084 canvas 50; https://www.digitale-sammlungen.de/de/view/bsb00060084?page=50)
- Claim supported: Everyone who gets the permission marries, however poor; many women give birth 14 to 16 times, and 6, 8, or 10 births are the rule; childless marriages are rare.
- German (ink): "Selten trifft man einen Mann, der Abneigung gegen die Ehe hätte, und jeder heirathet, welcher die Bewilligung zum heirathen erhält und wenn er noch so arm ist und das künftige Elend seiner Ehe schon voraussieht. [...] Es gibt viele Weiber die 14 bis 16 mal gebären u 6–8–10 mal gebären in der Regel alle. [...] Kinderlose Ehen sind selten."
- English: "One seldom meets a man who has an aversion to marriage, and everyone marries who receives the permission to marry, even if he is ever so poor and already foresees the future misery of his marriage. [...] There are many women who give birth 14 to 16 times, and as a rule all give birth 6–8–10 times. [...] Childless marriages are rare."
- Corrections against the machine text: "Chalosigkeit" -> "Ehelosigkeit"; "einem Mann" -> "einen Mann"; "Erhalt" -> "erhält"; "Che", "Chen" -> "Ehe", "Ehen"; "Zwillingsgeborfen" -> "Zwillingsgeburten". The numbers agree with the machine text.
- Confidence: high (every word read)
- Caveats: The ink has "6–8–10", so "6 to 10 times" is a fair summary but not the literal text. The "14" is written with the two digits joined; the crossbar of the 4 is visible, so I read 14 and not 11. The numbers are the physician's impression, not a count. The first omission is the heading "Fruchtbarkeit" and the sentence "Die Fruchtbarkeit der Weiber des hiesigen Gerichts Bezirkes ist sehr groß."; the second is "Bei den vielen Geburten kommen daher auch öfters Zwillingsgeburten vor."

## Summary

All six claims hold on the page images. None failed.

- Viechtach: holds. The physician blames the municipal veto for the illegitimate births, with hedging ("wie es scheint").
- Hersbruck: holds, with one important correction. The ink says "kein Unterkommen" (no livelihood), not "kein Unternehmen".
- Marktheidenfeld: holds. One word outside the key sentence is uncertain. The physician describes emigration as rare, and the ink has "fränkischen Amerikanerinnen".
- Windsheim: holds. The ages 30–40 and 25–30 are clear.
- Neustadt an der Waldnaab: holds. The sentence starts on canvas 137.
- Nittenau: holds. The ink has "14 bis 16 mal" and "6–8–10 mal".

Limit for all six: each passage is the opinion or estimate of one district physician. None gives a count.

Strongest single hook: Marktheidenfeld. "Wird einst den Armen das Heurathen erlaubt werden, so wird es keine fränkischen Amerikanerinnen mehr geben." Couples who were refused a marriage permit at home kept their promise by crossing the Atlantic to marry. The Viechtach sentence about enemies on the council and feared rogues is the best second quote, because it shows how the veto worked in practice.
